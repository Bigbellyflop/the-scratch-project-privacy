The Scratch Project

Support

The Scratch Project is a golf improvement app for the mid-handicap player working toward scratch: round stats against scratch benchmarks, a practice lab, skill assessments, a wedge matrix, pre-shot and warmup routines, and a notebook. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

Contact

Questions, problems or feedback: email pcrgolflabs@gmail.com. Please include your phone model and iOS version, and what you were doing when something went wrong.

Problem reports

If the app crashes, it keeps a report on your phone. Open the menu, choose Backup & Restore, and use the Problem Reports section to save the report and send it to us. Reports contain no personal data.

Your data

Your rounds, notes and videos never leave your device unless you export a backup yourself. See the privacy policy for the details.
