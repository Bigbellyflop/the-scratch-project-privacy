Privacy Policy

The Scratch Project · Last updated September 23, 2026

The Scratch Project is a golf app for logging rounds, practice and notes. It is built so that your information stays on your own phone. This policy explains what that means in practice.

What We Collect

Nothing. The app has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting service. Nothing you enter is ever sent to us or to anyone else.

The app makes one kind of network request: when you open the hole map, it fetches satellite imagery tiles for that hole from Esri (ArcGIS Location Platform). That request tells Esri which patch of ground to draw, which reveals roughly where you are playing, and includes nothing else about you. Tiles are kept on your phone for about a week so a hole you have viewed works without a connection. Esri's own privacy policy covers how they handle those requests. If you never open the hole map, the app makes no network connections at all.

What the App Stores on Your Phone

Everything you enter stays in the app's private storage on your device: rounds and scores, practice drills and sessions, yardages, routines, journal entries, settings, and any photos or swing videos you record or import. We cannot see any of it.

If the app crashes, it saves a short technical report on your device. You can view, save or delete these reports under Backup & Restore. They are only shared if you choose to send one yourself.

Camera, Microphone and Photos

The app asks for the camera to take photos for journal entries and drills and to record swings for the Swing Analyzer. It asks for the microphone so swing recordings include sound, which lets you hear impact when you review them. It can save an annotated swing frame to your photo library if you ask it to. Photos, videos and recordings stay on your device.

Location

The app asks for your precise location to show yardages to the green on the scorecard. Your position is compared with the green on your own course card and shown as a number; it is not recorded and it is not sent anywhere. If you use Center From My Position in the hole map's marker editor, the one position you were standing at is saved into that course card on your phone. You can decline the permission and the scorecard works without yardages.

Backups

You can export a backup file from Backup & Restore. Where that file goes is up to you.

Your phone's own backup service (Google backup on Android, iCloud on iPhone) may include the app's data as part of your regular device backup, stored in your own account under Google's or Apple's terms. On Android, that cloud backup includes the app's database and settings but not swing videos or photos. You can turn device backup off in your phone's settings.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any data, because we never receive any. Anything that leaves your phone, such as a backup file, a crash report or a saved image, leaves only because you chose to send or save it. The one exception is the hole map's imagery request described above, which goes to Esri and carries only the map area to draw.

Deleting Your Data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on your device. Backup files you exported and device backups are kept until you delete them.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer contact email shown on the app's Google Play or App Store listing.
